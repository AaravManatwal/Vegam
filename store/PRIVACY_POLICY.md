# Vegam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Vegam ("the app") is a download manager. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What we collect
**Nothing is sent to us.** Vegam has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no crash-reporting service. We do not operate a server that receives your data.

## What the app stores on your device
- The links (URLs) you add, file names, sizes, progress and status, so downloads can resume. This is kept in a local database on your device.
- Your settings (theme, speed limit, number of connections, etc.).
- Downloaded files, in the app's own folder or in a folder you choose.

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the app's own data; files you saved to a folder you chose remain there.

## Network
The app connects only to the web addresses you ask it to download. Those websites can see your IP address and standard request information, as with any browser. We do not control those websites; please read their privacy policies.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state**: to download files and check connectivity.
- **Notifications (Android 13+)**: to show download progress and a Pause button.
- **Foreground service (data sync)**: to keep downloads running while the app is in the background.
- **Folder access**: only the folder you pick through the system file picker.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
